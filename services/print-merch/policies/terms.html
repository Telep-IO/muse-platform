Artwork rights

You must attest that you own or are licensed for every design you upload before checkout. Telep IO LLC is solely responsible to Printify for content submitted through this shop. Do not upload artwork you do not have the right to print.

Pricing

The price is the live Printify product cost, plus the live shipping cost, plus a configured markup (25% unless MARKUP_BPS is changed). Prices are calculated when the draft is created. Demo mode uses a labeled fixture and does not call Printify.

Production

The shop's order approval must be Manual. An order is sent to production only after Stripe reports payment_status paid. White-label shipping means Printify is not asked to email the recipient a ship notice. Production time varies by print provider. API-created products skip Printify's standard quality check; review the mockups before you pay.

Cancellation

Printify accepts cancellation only while the order is on-hold or payment-not-received. Later statuses cannot be canceled here.

Demo

Demo mode is a stub. It does not create a Printify product, charge a card, or submit an order.
