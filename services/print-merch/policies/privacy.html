PrintMerch privacy

Telep IO LLC operates PrintMerch. This notice is plain language, not legal advice. Telep IO is independent of Meta.

What we collect

  • Product choices, artwork URL, mockup URLs, quantity, and the quoted price.
  • Recipient name, email, phone, and shipping address — sent to Printify only after you pay.
  • Stripe payment status and checkout identifiers. We do not store full card numbers.
  • API key identifiers and standard host logs.

What we do with it

Demo mode keeps drafts in a local database and does not contact Printify or Stripe. In test and live mode, artwork and the shipping address are sent to Printify to create a product, mockups, and — only after Stripe reports a paid checkout — a production order. Printify prints and ships the goods. Shipping notifications from Printify to the recipient are turned off; Telep IO LLC is the merchant of record.

Retention

Payment-adjacent rows (drafts, checkout sessions, and fulfillment claims) are stored in the fulfillment database for that APP_MODE. Demo gateway drafts use a separate SQLite file. They are not kept in an in-memory map.

Keys

Bearer API keys authenticate agents. Treat them as secrets.
