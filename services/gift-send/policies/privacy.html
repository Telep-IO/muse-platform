GiftSend privacy

Effective 24 September 2026. GiftSend is operated by Telep IO LLC. Contact: jon@telep.io. This is a product notice, not legal advice.

What GiftSend does

GiftSend drafts a digital gift card, Visa or Mastercard prepaid reward, or charity reward. A person reviews the draft and pays the face value plus a service fee through Stripe. Tremendous then delivers the reward by email, text, or link. Demo mode is a stub and does not contact Tremendous or Stripe.

What we collect

  • Recipient name, email, and/or phone number, the reward id, amount, personal message, and delivery method.
  • The Stripe payment reference, Tremendous order id, reward id, and delivery link after a paid order.
  • Standard web logs (IP, user agent, request path) from the host.

We do not sell personal information or run advertising pixels. We do not receive full card numbers. Cash-payout destinations (Venmo, PayPal, ACH, bank) are not collected in this version.

What we do with it

In test and live mode, listing products is a read-only Tremendous catalog call. Creating a draft does not place a Tremendous order. After Stripe reports payment_status paid, recipient and reward details are sent to Tremendous so it can deliver the reward. Demo mode keeps the draft in this service's local store and does not send it to Tremendous or Stripe.

Retention

Paid drafts and claim rows are kept so a retry cannot place a second Tremendous order and so delivery status can be read back. Contact jon@telep.io about access or deletion. Deletion cannot recall a reward the recipient has already redeemed.

Keys

Bearer API keys authenticate agents on the gateway. Treat them as secrets. Telep IO can revoke a key by removing it from the gateway configuration. This service may also require a separate service token. Tremendous and Stripe secrets stay in the host's secret store.
