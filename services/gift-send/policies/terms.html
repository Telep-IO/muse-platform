The service

An agent may draft a digital gift card or prepaid reward. A person reviews it and pays through Stripe. Tremendous delivers the reward only after a webhook with payment_status paid. Drafting does not move funds.

GiftSend is offered on the Tremendous Platform Client track. Telep IO LLC must be a registered Platform Client. Self-serve API credentials are not a license to resell rewards. This page does not claim that registration is finished.

Gift cards, prepaid, and charity only

Launch scope is digital gift cards, Visa and Mastercard prepaid rewards, and charity. Venmo, PayPal, ACH, bank, and other cash payouts are disabled. Enabling them would require a separate money-transmission review. Tremendous's published fee on the allowed categories is $0. Monetary options are 4–6% and are not offered here.

Pricing and funding

The customer total is the reward face value plus a service fee (default $2.99). Face value is a pass-through of the Tremendous catalog amount. The Tremendous balance that pays the reward is prefunded by bank ACH. This service does not auto-fund that balance by credit card.

Cancellation

A reward can be cancelled before the recipient redeems it. Tremendous returns HTTP 422 when a reward is already redeemed, and GiftSend reports that refusal as-is. The Tremendous API also limits cancellation of rewards that have not failed delivery. Contract language allows cancellation until redemption or 7 days, subject to what the cancel endpoint accepts. The service fee is not refunded after Tremendous has accepted the order. Demo cancels do not contact Tremendous.

Delivery

Delivery methods are email, phone (SMS), or a link the agent relays. Scheduling a future send date is supported by the Tremendous create-order field deliver_at (a date within the next year; time-of-day is ignored). GiftSend v1 does not expose scheduling. Rewards send when payment succeeds.

Limits

$2,000.00 maximum per payout. $10,000.00 maximum per recipient per rolling day. Amounts above either cap are rejected.

Demo

Demo mode is a stub. It returns a sample catalog and a local checkout URL. It performs no Tremendous or Stripe calls, including the free sandbox. A stub draft is not a sent reward.
