A few good things to know.

What happens after I pay?

We send your approved document and addresses to our printing partner, Lob. They print, envelope, and hand your letter to USPS. Your order page confirms when the printer accepts the order; that is not confirmation of delivery.

Can I cancel or change my letter?

Check everything before paying. Orders may enter production immediately, so edits and cancellations aren’t guaranteed. If the printer rejects your order before accepting it, we initiate a refund. If a provider response is uncertain, we review it before sending again.

What happens to my document?

Your document is available only through your private order link. Unpaid drafts expire after 48 hours. We remove completed order documents and addresses from our active storage after 30 days. Our payment and print providers have their own retention policies. Read our privacy notice.
