What we receive and why

We receive uploaded documents, filenames, sender and recipient names and postal addresses, and order instructions, either directly from you or from an integration acting on your instructions. Documents may contain personal information about you and other people. Provide only information necessary for your letter that you are authorized to share.

We use this information to prepare previews, check addresses, process orders, print and mail letters, provide support, investigate misuse, and meet legal obligations. We also store page counts, prices, order status, provider references, payment totals and tax, approval timestamps, policy versions, and cryptographic fingerprints of the approved document and mailing details. These fingerprints are verification records, not a claim that the data is anonymous.

When information is shared

In test or live mode, when you select “Prepare my letter,” we send the street, city, state, and ZIP details of both addresses to Lob for an internal deliverability check, before payment. We do not expose Lob's address-verification report as a separate product. After successful payment, Lob receives your prepared document, sender and recipient names and addresses, and an order reference. Lob and its printing and postal partners use them for fulfillment. The recipient receives the physical document and the return-address information.

Stripe receives checkout and transaction information and collects payment, billing, and contact information as needed. PaperSend does not receive full card numbers. Our private order return link is included in Stripe's checkout configuration. Our hosting and backup providers process stored service data. See Lob's privacy policy and Stripe's privacy policy for their practices.

An integration receives the draft result and private order link and can retrieve the order using its access token. The integration provider's handling of its conversations and files is governed by its own policy. In demo mode, PaperSend makes no calls to Stripe or Lob, but documents and addresses are still stored using the demo's configured storage providers.

For our Neon-backed deployment, Neon processes order records, mailing addresses while retained, payment/refund references, and approval evidence. PDFs and preview images are stored separately on our hosting server, not in Neon. Database restore history and hosting backups may retain earlier copies after active records are deleted. See Neon's privacy policy.

Access, security, and tracking

A private order link contains a bearer credential: anyone who has it can view the order and document while retained. Protect the link and avoid sharing it publicly. Authorized personnel and providers may access relevant information for fulfillment, support, security, abuse investigation, or legal requirements. We do not routinely have a person review every document's contents.

The application uses access controls and limits request volume. It uses network addresses temporarily for rate limiting. Hosting, proxy, and payment providers may also process technical logs or essential cookies under their own policies. PaperSend does not include advertising trackers, sell personal information, or use documents to train AI models. These statements concern PaperSend's own practices and do not describe every independent provider's practices. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Successful preparation replaces the original upload with a rasterized print PDF and page previews. Unpaid drafts expire after 48 hours. A background job removes their documents and addresses and removes those of completed or refunded orders after 30 days. Orders awaiting payment reconciliation, printer confirmation, or investigation remain until resolved. Cleanup runs periodically rather than at an exact instant.

Approval fingerprints, wording, policy versions, and timestamps are kept until at least 180 days after order creation and until the order is resolved and its document has been removed. The approval record does not separately duplicate postal addresses or document contents. Minimal order and transaction references may be retained for accounting, disputes, and legal obligations. Provider records, infrastructure backups, and legally required preservation have separate retention periods; removal from active storage does not immediately erase every backup or provider copy.
